The association

Who we are

River Plate Copenhague is a community of Club Atlético River Plate supporters based in Copenhagen, Denmark. We are a non-profit voluntary association founded by Argentine fans of the club.

We organise sporting and social activities and bring River Plate supporters together across Denmark.
